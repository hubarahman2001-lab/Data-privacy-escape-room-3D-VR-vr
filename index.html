Lock Down the Office

It's 7:45 pm and you came back for your charger. A visitor badge went missing today, so Security is sealing the building in 15 minutes. Walk the route from the car park to the server room, find and fix every privacy risk with your own hands, collect the four-digit all-clear code and enter it before the doors lock.

How to play
  • Hands (Quest): pinch to pick things up, poke buttons, reach, push and turn things the way you would for real. Nobody tells you which move to use: work it out. A small menu above your left wrist has 💡 Hint and 🔁 Again.
  • Controllers: trigger = pinch, grip = fist, A/X = open hand, B/Y = hint. Left stick walks, right stick turns.
  • Screen: click to pick, drag to carry, hold the mouse on something to hold your hand there. Drag empty space to look around, WASD to walk, H for a hint, P to pause.
  • 3 hints per run. Wrong choices cost 10 seconds, harmless things you flag cost 5. Pass mark: 80% of choices right first time.
  • Give yourself about 2 × 2 m of clear space. You'll take a step or two to reach some things.
